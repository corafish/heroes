Tier List Maker

Between games, my friends and I love playing what we call the "Headcanon Game," where we assign headcanons to all the characters based on a prompt. Sometimes they are unique qualities assigned to each character and sometimes they're a position in a tier list. I don't like using tier list sites, which are often out of date and require sign‑ins to make/edit lists, so I made my own here! This tier list maker includes all 54 current heroes, and I plan to update it as new heroes are added.

Instructions: Click the character portraits and drag them to a tier. When you're done, use your native screen-capture tool to take a screenshot and save it to your computer/phone. To reset the list, refresh this page. If you're not sure who a character is, you can hover over their portrait to see their name. The title text and tier names can be edited!!

Click the button for a prompt to inspire you!
